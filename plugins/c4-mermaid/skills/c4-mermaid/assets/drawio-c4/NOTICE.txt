The local adapters freeze C4 styles and paint geometry as data, parameterize dimensions, keep the person head circular, reserve safe label areas, and adapt font contrast. File/object stores use the C4 data-container cylinder; general applications and components use the C4 rounded blocks. No optional external icon/theme asset is required.
